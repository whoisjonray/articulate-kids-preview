How to get started

Three simple steps.

No waiting lists. No referrals. No complicated processes. Just book online and we'll take care of everything else.

1

Book your assessment

Select a preschool age assessment (under 5, not yet at school) or a school age assessment (5 and over). Fees are shown at booking.

2

Add your lab tests

Add neurological and gut health tests at the same time or after your assessment. Most parents tell us it's the best decision they made.

3

Confirm and pay

You'll receive an email confirmation, a payment link, and full instructions for your lab test. Our team is with you every step.
