How to get started

Three simple steps.

No waiting lists. No referrals. No complicated processes. Just book online and we'll take care of everything else.

STEP 01

Book your assessment

Choose a preschool assessment (under 5, not yet at school) or a school-age assessment (5 and over). Fees are shown as you book.

STEP 02

Add your lab tests

Add neurological and gut health tests at the same time, or after your assessment. Most parents call it the best decision they made.

STEP 03

Confirm and pay

You'll get an email confirmation, a payment link and full test instructions. Someone from our team is with you at every step.
