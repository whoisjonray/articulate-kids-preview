Why these numbers, and what I need from you

Your £90 is not the problem. It's right at the going rate. West London charge £88 for 45 minutes, ISLTS £85 an hour, Children's Wellness Centre £70 to £95. What they do differently is charge for everything that isn't the session. West London bill the progress report at £125, a school observation at £40 per half hour, a meeting at £40 per half hour, and a home programme at £80 an hour, all on top. That's the work you're currently doing for free, and it's why you feel like you're on £45.

So the fix isn't a cheaper bundle. A discounted block would make it worse. The fix is to put the between-session work inside the price and sell the block as one thing.

  1. The £795 block, checked against the market: the same thing at West London comes to about £773 once a parent adds the report, a home programme and one school call, plus travel. So £795 all-in is competitive and simpler for the parent.
  2. What it does for you: today 6 sessions bring in £540 for roughly 12 hours of your time once the admin is counted, which is the £45 you described. At £795 the same block is about £66 an hour. Not perfect, but roughly a 45% rise without pricing yourself out.
  3. Single sessions go up to £110 and are for existing families only. That's deliberate. It makes the block the obvious buy and stops the ad-hoc bookings that create most of the admin.

Three things I can't fill in without you: (1) what you currently charge for an assessment, so £295 is either right or gets replaced; (2) what the Neural Zoomer and Gut Zoomer panels cost you, so the Whole Child price is real and not a guess; (3) whether 6 sessions or 8 is the right block for how you actually work. The clinics I looked at are split between the two.

Four things on this page I made up, because you don't have a stated policy on them. Confirm, change or delete each one and I'll update it: the free 15-minute call at the bottom (ISLTS offer a free 20-minute one and it's a good filter for you, but you don't currently offer anything like it); payment in three monthly instalments; unused sessions staying live for six months; and 48 hours' notice to move an appointment. None of these should go live as written until you've said yes to them.

Before you ask

The things parents worry about.

Do I have to commit to a block?

If we assess your child and therapy isn't what they need, we'll tell you. Plenty of families come once and that's the end of it.

Why six sessions?

Because one or two won't move anything. Six weeks is roughly where change starts to hold, and you get a written summary at the end so you can decide what's next.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes. A block can be paid in three monthly instalments. Just ask when you book.

What if we need to stop?

Life happens. Unused sessions stay on your account for six months, and you can move an appointment with 48 hours' notice.

Not sure which one?

Start with a free call. Fifteen minutes with Hulya, no pressure, and you'll leave knowing what your child actually needs.
